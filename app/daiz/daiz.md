# DAIZLAB

## Engineering human intent into digital systems.

DaizLab is an AI-supported digital ecosystem founded and architected by **Oluwadamilare Taofeek Oloyede**, publicly known as **Damiedaiz**.

DaizLab explores how human intent can be turned into digital systems through software, artificial intelligence, automation, infrastructure and increasingly autonomous workflows.

## The Ecosystem

### OPMMA
**One Person Marketing & Management Agency**

A DaizLab operating framework for enabling one person to market, manage and operate a digital business through systems, AI, automation and infrastructure.

### DaizSystems
Systems and infrastructure for engineering digital business outcomes.

### DaizFi
A Daiz ecosystem layer exploring identity, contribution and financial-system architecture.

### DaizAI
AI-oriented systems within the Daiz ecosystem.

### PidGinYor
An experimental language/developer system within the Daiz ecosystem.

### A.I.R.
AI Interpretation Readiness: a framework concerned with making digital entities and systems easier for AI systems to interpret.
